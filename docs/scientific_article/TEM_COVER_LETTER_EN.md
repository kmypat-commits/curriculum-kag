# Cover letter draft for TEM Journal

**Manuscript title:** Curriculum-KAG: Expert-Grounded and Constraint-Aware Curriculum Synthesis from Learning Outcomes

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript for publication in TEM Journal. The paper presents Curriculum-KAG, an implemented decision-support system for evidence-based curriculum synthesis. It combines the Kazakhstan EPVO programme repository, multilingual SBERT retrieval, expert-weighted course–learning-outcome evidence, prerequisite-aware graph checks, and a constrained planner.

The manuscript makes four contributions. First, it describes a provenance-preserving pipeline for transforming registry cards and expert checks into a normalized course–LO evidence layer. Second, it evaluates multilingual SBERT against a base model on an independent 6,000-pair test set. Third, it reports a frozen experiment in which expert-weighted retrieval improves ranking metrics. Fourth, it shows how hard credit, semester, domain, prerequisite, and protected-component constraints are enforced before an alternative plan is presented to an academic expert.

The operational system is intentionally human-supervised. The expert-weighted reranker is reported as an experimental second stage and is not enabled in production. GNN and LSTM pilots are reported as controlled research baselines and are not presented as deployed components. Structural comparisons with EPVO programmes are explicitly labelled as external-reference diagnostics rather than blinded educational efficacy studies.

The work is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by another journal or conference. The author has approved the submitted manuscript. No student, medical, or performance data were used. Raw registry records and large model checkpoints are not redistributed without authorization; derived manifests, checksums, scripts, and evaluation reports are provided for reproducibility.

The author declares no conflict of interest and no external funding. Generative AI was used only for software-development assistance and language editing; all algorithms, citations, numerical results, and conclusions were checked by the author, and no AI system made the final academic approval decision.

Thank you for considering this manuscript.
