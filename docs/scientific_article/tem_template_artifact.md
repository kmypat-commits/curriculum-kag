## Content slots

1. Title and subtitle.
2. Author names, affiliations, corresponding-author email.
3. Abstract and up to five keywords.
4. Numbered Introduction, Related Work/Background, Methodology, Results, Discussion and Conclusion sections.
5. Acknowledgements (optional), references and corresponding-author block.
6. One-column figures with captions below; two-column figures are allowed when required.
