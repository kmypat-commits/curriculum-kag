# Code of Conduct

Curriculum-KAG contributors are expected to communicate professionally, evaluate ideas on evidence, respect disciplinary and cultural differences, and avoid disclosing institutional or personal data.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, publication of private information and deliberate manipulation of research evidence are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these principles.

Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is specific, reproducible and respectful. Concerns can be reported privately through the repository owner or GitHub security/contact mechanisms.
